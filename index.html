About

Vincent Pham is a 16-year-old violinist from Ottawa whose experience spans solo performance, chamber music and concertmaster leadership. He has been praised in masterclasses for his musicality and his analytical approach to music, particularly his attention to a piece’s structure and phrasing and how these elements shape his interpretation.

He is in the studio of Prof. Michael Van der Sloot and attended the Conservatoire de musique de Gatineau with Marjolaine Lambert and Tobie Slippert. He studied at Juilliard Summer Music in New York in 2026 and was selected to perform in the highlight concert. In 2025, at age 15, he was a top prize winner in the Strings 15 to 18 category of the Canadian Music Competition. He also holds the Associate Diploma (ARCT) from the Royal Conservatory of Music.
