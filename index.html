About

Vincent Pham is a 16-year-old violinist from Ottawa, Ontario, and a student in the International Baccalaureate program. He studies in the studio of Prof. Van der Sloot, is enrolled at the Conservatoire de musique de Gatineau and the University of Ottawa Pre-College Program, and holds the Associate Diploma (ARCT) from the Royal Conservatory of Music.

In 2025 he placed third in the Strings 15 to 18 category of the Canadian Music Competition, competing at age 15. He has appeared as soloist and concertmaster with the Conservatoire de musique de Gatineau and the Vyhovskyi Senior Strings, and plays chamber music in a quartet that won the Aruna-Rupa Anantaraman Trophy for Senior Chamber in 2026. In summer 2026 he attended Juilliard Summer Music in New York, with private lessons, masterclasses and a highlight concert performance.

For more than five years, Vincent has performed in concerts around Ottawa that have raised over $20,000 for local food banks and refugee initiatives. Away from music, he races dragon boats and is training for the Ottawa Marathon.

Beyond the violin

He also builds software and does research. He has made a multi-level tank game, a statistics and regression grapher, and a geometry calculator, and produced an educational video on the human immune response.
